# data/

This folder is not tracked by Git, except this file. Copy the inputs from the team Drive (Data/AI folder) into the paths below.

| Folder | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `in_use/` | Read by the pipeline today |
| `next/` | Will be used once the open decisions are settled |
| `reference/` | Not read by any code: comparisons, old versions, plans, previews |
| `output/` | Generated by `python -m pipeline.run_all` |

## in_use/

| File | From | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `buildings_osm_ghsl.geojson` | Data/AI | 86,519 OSM buildings with `area_m2`, `levels`, `height_m`, and the GHSL columns `ghsl_anbh_mean` (height), `ghsl_age_majority` and `ghsl_age_period` (epoch). ## Known limits

- Only about 28% of the 520 UNOSAT points fall within 10 m of a building of 400 m2 or larger (22% fall inside one). Most damage points are on smaller buildings.
- The raster and the typology cover Saltivka only, while `buildings.geojson` covers the whole city.
- The pipeline uses the newer GHSL version (`final_data`). The older version disagrees on height and epoch. Data/AI still has to confirm where the height and epoch come from.

## Source
